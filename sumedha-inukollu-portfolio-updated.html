Ramble2Reel

Everyone records voice notes and almost nobody listens to them again. Ramble2Reel turns a messy one-to-two minute ramble into a 30 to 60 second vertical video, ready for Reels, Shorts or WhatsApp status.

  1. Whisper transcribes the note locally, with a timestamp for every word.

  2. Claude turns the ramble into a hook, a body and a takeaway. An offline cleaner takes over when there's no API key.

  3. Use a neural narrator, or keep your own voice with the fillers, stutters and long pauses cut out and every caption re-timed to match.

  4. Music is composed in code to the exact length, and ducks automatically whenever someone is speaking.

  5. Captions light up word by word over visuals that pulse with the voice. Out comes an MP4, a cover image and SRT subtitles.

2026, knowledge engineering

PedALL-KB

An LLM-assisted knowledge base for pediatric acute lymphoblastic leukaemia

Turns scattered research evidence into structured, standardised and traceable data, then answers questions with citations back to the source records.

  • Pulls biomarker, variant and drug evidence from PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, CIViC, OncoKB, ChEMBL and UniProt
  • Hybrid rule-based NER plus LLM extraction, validated against a hand-labelled gold set
  • Genes, diseases and drugs mapped to HGNC, MONDO and RxNorm identifiers
  • Automated data-quality checks built on FAIR principles
  • Gene–disease–drug knowledge graph with a RAG question-answering layer

Life Sciences Sales Copilot

AI-powered call preparation for pharmaceutical sales teams

Helps a sales rep prepare for a meeting with a healthcare professional, by combining clinical-trial evidence with that doctor's call history.

  • Retrieves relevant evidence from ClinicalTrials.gov and CRM-style call history with semantic search
  • Writes a structured, source-grounded preparation sheet using Gemini 3.6 Flash
